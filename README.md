# Wasim Ali - Portfolio Website

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no installs.

## Run it on your computer
Double-click `index.html`. (Google Fonts need internet; the site still works offline with fallback fonts.)

## Edit your details (5 minutes)
1. `js/config.js`: your CV email is configured; add a Formspree form ID if you want direct form delivery.
2. `index.html`: update your services, experience, education and skills when your CV changes.
3. Your portrait is linked from the project folder as `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-06 at 1.30.26 AM.jpeg`. To change it, update the image path in the hero section of `index.html`.

## Replace sample work with real projects
The work section contains interactive showcases for the CodeAlpha digital marketing internship (`codealpha-internship.html`), sales dashboard (`interactive-data-dashboard.html`), Asynclick social media management (`social-media-management.html`), Jubilee Micro Solutions promotion and customer communication (`product-promotion-customer-communication.html`), and Thread & Glow GB startup brand marketing (`thread-glow-brand-marketing.html`). The CodeAlpha showcase links to the three submitted reports in `assets/codealpha/`. Dashboard figures, social posts, offers, product details, prices, names and sample plans are illustrative and do not claim campaign results.

The reviews section is intentionally unscored until you have genuine client feedback. Add quotes or ratings only with the client's permission and never present invented reviews as real.

## Make the contact form work (free)
Option A (easiest): leave `formspreeId` empty. The form opens the visitor's email app.
Option B: create a free account at formspree.io, create a form, paste its ID in `js/config.js`.

## Publish for free
**Netlify Drop (fastest, 2 minutes):** go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the whole `wasim-portfolio` folder onto the page. You get a live link instantly; rename it in Site settings.
**GitHub Pages:** create a GitHub repo, upload all files, then Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch `main` / root. Your site appears at `https://USERNAME.github.io/REPO/`.
**Cloudflare Pages:** connect the repo or upload the folder; no build command needed.
Add the live link to your Upwork profile and proposals.
