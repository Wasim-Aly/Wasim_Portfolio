My workflow

Content calendar (sample month)

Brand consistency

  • One palette across every post
  • Consistent type and layout templates
  • Same logo placement and spacing
Tone of voice
  • Friendly and clear, never pushy
  • Short sentences, one idea per post
  • Always end with a simple call to action
